Project 02 / CMOS Circuits

CMOS logic, at transistor level.

This self-project is documented in the existing portfolio. The circuit artwork shown here is a conceptual illustration—not a captured LTspice schematic or waveform.

Overview

Boolean functions in CMOS.

Designed and simulated NOT, AND, OR, NAND, NOR, XOR, and XNOR logic gates using NMOS/PMOS transistor-level circuits and LTspice.

The recorded workflow includes transient waveform analysis and checking the output response against expected logic behaviour.

  • LTspice
  • NMOS / PMOS
  • CMOS
  • Transient simulation

Illustrative circuit

Concept illustration; no project photo was included with the source files.

Gate families

NOT · AND · OR · NAND · NOR · XOR · XNOR

Learning outcomes

Transistor-level CMOS logic, NMOS/PMOS operation, transient simulation, and interpreting voltage waveforms.
