01 / About

Curious about what happens beneath the silicon.

I’m Mayank Saini, a B.Tech student in VLSI Design & Technology at B.K. Birla Institute of Engineering & Technology, Pilani. My portfolio documents a growing focus on digital design, RTL, and verification.

My introduction

From digital logic to processor architecture.

I’m drawn to the connection between a specification and the hardware that implements it. My academic and project work has included Verilog and SystemVerilog, combinational and sequential logic, FSMs, RTL simulation, and waveform-based debugging.

My documented work spans transistor-level CMOS logic gates and an 8-bit RISC-style processor. I enjoy learning how individual hardware blocks fit together into a reliable system.

What I value

Build, simulate, understand.

Digital electronics, computer architecture, semiconductor devices, and the broader VLSI design flow are areas I’m keen to explore further. I’m particularly interested in the discipline of making hardware behaviour precise and testable.

My career goal is to continue developing toward VLSI design and verification roles. The learning roadmap distinguishes documented experience from future topics I plan to study.
